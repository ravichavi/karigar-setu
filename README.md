# 🧵 KarigarSetu

### From Craft to Commerce

KarigarSetu is a mobile application designed to help artisans and micro-entrepreneurs digitally showcase, manage, and market their handmade products with minimal technical effort.

The application simplifies the process of converting a physical handmade product into a professional digital product listing.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Many artisans and micro-entrepreneurs have high-quality handmade products but face difficulties in:

- Creating professional product listings
- Taking and presenting product photographs
- Writing product descriptions
- Deciding suitable prices
- Managing digital product catalogues
- Reaching larger digital markets
- Using complex digital platforms due to limited technical knowledge

KarigarSetu aims to make this process simple and accessible.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

KarigarSetu acts as a **digital business assistant for artisans**.

An artisan can upload a product photo and provide basic product information. The application then provides an AI-assisted workflow for creating a professional product listing.
